Recipe Finder Support

Scan your fridge, get recipes you can cook tonight.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Email amir.m.kabiri@gmail.com and include your device and iOS version.

Free and Pro

  • Free: 3 starter scans, then 1 scan a week, plus 3 typed recipe searches a week.
  • Pro: daily scans, many more recipe searches, and a photo of each dish.

Subscriptions

To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. If you reinstalled the app, tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen.

Scanning tips

  • Use good light and open the fridge door fully.
  • Take a second photo of the door shelves or pantry if needed.
  • Review the ingredient list and add anything the scan missed.
